**September 2026.** `malcom.evaluation/fdr_results.json` holds machine-checked FDR
refinement verdicts for the three archived May 2026 AUV runs, generated by
`scripts/run_fdr_verdicts.sh` on a licensed FDR 4.2.7 through the pipeline's own gate
(`behaviour_assembly` + `fdr4`).

| archived run | verdict |
|---|---|
| `web_20260530_182240_auto1` | **verified** — all 6 deadlock/divergence assertions hold |
| `web_20260530_191159_auto2` | **not assemblable** — archived model does not parse as RoboChart |
| `web_20260530_195147_auto3` | **not assemblable** — same parse error |

## The disclosure

Runs 2 and 3's archived behaviour models (`STM.txt`) are rejected by the official
RoboChart parser (`XtextSyntaxDiagnostic: mismatched input ',' expecting '}'` — a
comma-separated `uses Inputs, Outputs` clause the grammar does not accept). They
therefore **cannot have been FDR-checked in their archived form.**

This sits in tension with the May sweep's recorded 10/10 FDR pass rate. The honest
readings are:

1. the text that was checked in May differed from what was archived (e.g. a
   normalisation step that has since changed), or
2. the May FDR path was more permissive than the current assembler, or
3. the May verdicts for those runs were fail-soft passes — the check never ran and the
   gate accepted anyway, which was the default behaviour on any machine where the
   toolchain did not resolve (see the fail-soft audit findings and commit `4d0c4db`).

The repository does not carry evidence to separate these. What is machine-checked and
reproducible today is exactly what `fdr_results.json` records: one of three archived
behaviour models verifies; the paper's walkthrough run (auto1) is that one.

Reproduce with: `./scripts/run_fdr_verdicts.sh` (requires a licensed FDR4 and the built
MALCOMj distribution).
